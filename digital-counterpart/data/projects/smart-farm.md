# Smart Farm and Its Ecosystem

Smart Farm and Its Ecosystem, also called the Autonomous Nursery, began at
Budhanilkantha School in 2023. It started with two people and later grew into a
larger robotics group.

The approximately 60 cm by 30 cm by 25 cm prototype combined embedded systems,
IoT, sensors, automation, and machine learning. Arduino and Raspberry Pi
controlled camera-based data collection, temperature, humidity, soil-moisture
and gas sensing, relays, water pumps, drip irrigation, filament-bulb heating,
fan-based cross-ventilation, and a cloud-connected monitoring and control
dashboard.

The sensors let the nursery sense its physical environment. Control software
closed the loop by adjusting irrigation, heating, and ventilation.

A plant-disease classification model connected the physical automation work to
machine learning. Seeing a trained model solve a physical-world problem was one
of the experiences that pushed me to study AI more seriously.
