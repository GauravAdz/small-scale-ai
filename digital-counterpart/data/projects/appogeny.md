# Appogeny

Appogeny is a public web product I created for turning personal words, photos,
music, and small interactions into a single interactive page for someone. I
started building it in July 2026.

## The Experience

Instead of sending a long message or a conventional digital greeting card, a
creator shapes the message into chapters that the recipient moves through. The
creation flow starts with the occasion and feeling, builds a first draft from
selected chapters, lets the creator preview and personalise the result, and
then publishes it for sharing.

## Accounts, Sharing, and Privacy

Appogeny deliberately does not require user accounts. Publishing creates two
separate links:

- an unlisted recipient link that opens the finished page; and
- a private edit link that lets the creator change or delete it.

Recipient pages are unlisted rather than publicly listed or indexed. The
product centres on one interactive page for one person, not a public profile or
social feed.

## Status and Use

The product is live at [appogeny.app](https://appogeny.app). It has had roughly
20 users, including people beyond my immediate circle.

I started Appogeny as a personal product project and am open to developing it
into a larger commercial product or startup if there is enough genuine user
interest.

## What Building It Taught Me

Appogeny pushed me to think beyond whether the code works. I had to consider
product design, onboarding, privacy trade-offs, interaction design, sharing
flows, and whether someone who did not build the product could understand and
enjoy using it.
