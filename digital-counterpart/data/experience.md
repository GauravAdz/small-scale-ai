# Experience

## Overview

My professional experience is still early. It spans paid science teaching,
technical mentoring, student and robotics leadership, graphic design, and
independent AI and product development.

Much of my technical experience comes from building directly: identifying a
problem, making a working version, putting it in front of people, and improving
it from there. I am most interested in AI systems, intelligent automation, AI
agents, and software products that combine engineering with experimentation.

## Paid Employment

### Science Teacher — Budhanilkantha School

**December 2024 – May 2025**

**Kathmandu, Nepal · Paid employment · approximately 40–45 hours per week**

After graduating from Budhanilkantha School, I returned as a Science Teacher. I
taught integrated science to students in Grades 5–8, covering physics,
chemistry, and biology. I designed my own lessons and focused on helping
students understand scientific concepts rather than simply reproduce material.

### Makerspace and Robotics Mentoring

Alongside normal classes, I contributed to the school's makerspace because of
my previous robotics experience. I mentored students, including A-Level
students, in:

- Arduino and Raspberry Pi
- Python
- Robotics, electronics, motors, and embedded systems
- Drones and their components
- Laser cutting
- Technical project development

I supervised student projects, helped students prepare for exhibitions and
competitions, and accompanied them to competitions outside the school. I
mentored roughly 20 students through makerspace and robotics activities while
working alongside the makerspace staff. Projects built by students I worked
with included drones and a tomato-classification system.

Teaching showed me that explaining a subject is one of the clearest tests of
whether I understand it. Sharing technical knowledge also made me more
confident in areas I had previously learned mainly through experimentation.

## Robotics Team Leadership

### Co-founder, Team Lead & Technical Lead — Team Knowledge Park

**July 2023 – mid-2024**

**Budhanilkantha School, Nepal**

I co-founded Team Knowledge Park in July 2023. The student robotics and
technology team began with a small core group and grew to around ten founding
members from AS and A Level.

My role combined team and technical leadership. I coordinated projects, divided
responsibilities, reviewed code and system logic, managed wiring and
electronics, trained newer members, and led preparation for science and
technology competitions.

We divided work according to people's strengths. Some members focused on
technical implementation; others were stronger at presenting projects,
creating posters, or explaining the ideas. Everyone presented and pitched part
of the team's work.

I also worked with school administrators to arrange resources, transport, and
participation in external events. We sourced components when necessary, with
the school reimbursing approved purchases and the makerspace providing
additional equipment and support.

The team's work included the Smart Farm and Its Ecosystem, also called the
Autonomous Nursery; Roomba-style robots; a machine intended to make maize-grain
separation easier; and other robotics, automation, and embedded-system
prototypes. The local, national, and international science and technology
exhibition achievements I earned during this period came through Team Knowledge
Park.

I became less active in leading the team around May–June 2024 as my final
examinations approached.

## Independent AI and Product Development

### Independent AI / Product Developer

**2026 – present**

**Linz, Austria**

I describe my current independent work as AI and product development. Since
moving to Austria, I have spent more time building complete software products,
particularly systems that combine AI with full-stack product development.

My main interests are AI agents, AI-assisted software development, intelligent
automation, retrieval systems, and practical LLM applications. Current work
includes Appogeny, the Digital Counterpart, and LinzSings; the earlier GauravGPT
project was the first experimental version of the personal-AI idea.

These projects have taught me that an AI product is more than a model call.
Retrieval, context, latency, caching, interface behaviour, reliability, and the
overall product experience matter just as much.

### LinzSings — Ars Electronica Festival 2026 AI Hackathon

In September 2026, I developed LinzSings during the official two-day AI
Hackathon at Ars Electronica Festival. The mobile web experience encodes City
of Linz street-record IDs inside music, decodes them through a phone's
microphone, and reveals the matching local story. The project won first prize
through the hackathon's community vote.

Building it brought together open civic data, browser audio processing,
data storytelling, and AI-assisted development under a short event deadline.

Graphic design is no longer my main focus, but it shaped how I think about

## How I Work

I learn best by building a small working version, testing it in reality, and
improving it from what I observe.

- **Shipping creates information.** A working version used by real people
  teaches me more than a long plan for something untested.
- **AI products are systems.** Retrieval, context, caching, latency,
  reliability, UX, and deployment all matter.
- **Teaching exposes gaps in understanding.** If I cannot explain a technical
  idea clearly, I probably do not understand it as well as I think.
- **Technical leadership involves translation.** Good projects match people to
  the work they are best at instead of expecting identical contributions.
- **Useful systems motivate me.** I am drawn to websites, automation, AI agents,
  and intelligent software more than content-production or social-media work.
